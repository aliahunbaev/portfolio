In *The War of Art*, Steven Pressfield describes the artist as a soldier at war with the Resistance.

The Resistance is everything that keeps you from honing your craft and bringing your gift into the world.

Most people quietly lose that battle, be it through externally imposed obstacles or internal battles with distraction, self-doubt, and fear.

This piece was designed as a daily reminder to fight in defense of your gifts. To stay vigilant against anything that dulls your focus or pulls you away from the work that matters.

The Soldier Uniform is intended to be worn wherever you train, be it the studio, library, gym — a simple staple that helps you maintain the mental frame of a soldier, defending your time, attention, and creativity by any means necessary.
