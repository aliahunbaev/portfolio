The idea for LockedIn started as an Instagram comment. It was on a post by @hardtokill making fun of LinkedIn. The comments were full of people venting about how soul-sucking their corporate lives had become.

I suggested creating an alternative — something more human. It turns out a lot of people are searching for exactly that.

![](/images/journal/workshirt/instagram-comments.jpg)

![](/images/journal/workshirt/new-quote-image.jpg)

Then Abdul came across this image on his feed. It captured a different attitude toward work — one rooted in love, focus, and gratitude for the role it plays in building the life you want. This quote, and the idea of a more fulfilling space called LockedIn, struck a chord with thousands.

Could we infuse that kind of energy into the corporate workday? That was the question behind The Workshirt.

Virgil Abloh often spoke about the 3% rule — the idea that altering an existing design by just 3% could create something entirely new.

We followed that principle, taking a classic white-collar shirt and making two subtle changes: a LockedIn logo on the front pocket, and the beautiful quote stitched onto the back collar.

![](/images/journal/workshirt/workshirt-design.jpg)

![](/images/journal/workshirt/front-pocket.jpg)

![](/images/journal/workshirt/back-collar.jpg)

These tiny details are meant as reminders to enjoy the process, take pride in focus, and approach your work with love.

Most people experience work as an obligation, something to get through in order to survive.

At its worst, it feels like a trap. At its best, it can be one of the greatest sources of joy in our lives.

Sometimes the difference simply relies on your approach – the ability to find meaning, to stay curious, to keep showing up.

If you've found work you love, cherish it. If you haven't yet, keep searching. You will.
